Sponsorships · how to help

How to sponsor us.

Your support helps the team build better robots, travel to competitions, and share STEM with more students. Four tiers, from $500 up to season naming rights. Equipment and mentorship count just as much.

01 · sponsorship tiers / ~/levels

Sponsorship tiers

Each tier includes everything in the tiers below it. Amounts are a starting point, not a ceiling. If you want to build something different, talk to us.

01 · Bronze

Bronze

from $500

Get your name in front of the team, the competition, and the community.

  • Logo & link on lebob.com.au
  • Logo on our pit display
  • Thank-you post on our socials
  • End-of-season report
02 · Silver

Silver

from $1,500

Everything in Bronze, plus a place on the kit we wear all season.

  • Logo on the season team shirt
  • Named in judging presentations
  • Named at outreach events
  • A dedicated post about the partnership
03 · Gold

Gold

from $3,000

Everything in Silver, plus prime placement and time with the team.

  • Prominent shirt placement
  • Logo on the robot, where the rules allow
  • A team demo at your workplace
  • Featured writeup & outreach materials
04 · Platinum

Platinum

from $5,000 · tailored

Everything in Gold, built around what your organisation actually wants.

  • Season or robot naming rights
  • Top billing across everything we make
  • Student site visits & careers sessions
  • Shaped with you, not off a list
02 · in-kind / ~/other-ways

Not everything useful is cash

Equipment, materials and expertise are worth just as much to us. In-kind support is recognised the same way: we'll place you at the tier that matches what you've given.

Equipment & materials

Tools, fabrication help, prototyping supplies, or printing resources.

  • 3D printer filament
  • Electronics components
  • Workshop supplies
Professional support

Volunteer mentorship in engineering, software, comms, or planning.

  • Robotics engineering
  • Soft robotics research
  • CAD & manufacturing
  • Presentation coaching
03 · impact / where it goes

Where your support goes

Registration

Competition registration fees

Parts

Prototype parts, sensors, replacement hardware

Outreach

Presentation materials and community outreach demos

Resources

Team learning resources and practice field upgrades

04 · the details / ~/fine-print

The details

  • All amounts are in AUD and exclude GST. Every sponsorship is invoiced, so it goes through your books as a marketing expense rather than a donation.
  • Shirt logos have to be locked in before the season print run. Ask us for this season's cutoff date before you commit to a Silver tier or above.
  • Logo placement on the robot is subject to the competition's branding rules, which set limits on size and position.
  • Tiers apply from the 2026–27 season onward. The organisations who backed us before we had a tier structure are listed permanently as Founding Supporters.
