Sponsorships · how to help

How to sponsor us.

Sponsors pay for the robot, the competition entries and the travel. Five tiers run from $500 up to a multi-season partnership we scope with you. Equipment and mentorship count for as much as cash.

01 · sponsorship tiers / ~/levels

Sponsorship tiers

Each tier includes the tiers below it. Treat the amounts as a floor. If you want to build something different, talk to us.

01 · Bronze

Bronze

from $500

Puts your name in front of the team and the competition.

  • Logo & link on lebob.com.au
  • Logo on our pit display
  • Thank-you post on our socials
  • End-of-season report
02 · Silver

Silver

from $1,500

Everything in Bronze, plus a place on the kit we wear all season.

  • Logo on the season team shirt
  • Named in judging presentations
  • Named at outreach events
  • A dedicated post about the partnership
03 · Gold

Gold

from $3,000

Everything in Silver, plus prime placement and time with the team.

  • Prominent shirt placement
  • Logo on the robot, where the rules allow
  • A team demo at your workplace
  • Featured writeup & outreach materials
04 · Platinum

Platinum

from $5,000 · tailored

Everything in Gold, built around what your organisation asks for.

  • Season or robot naming rights
  • Top billing across everything we make
  • Student site visits & careers sessions
  • Scoped with you rather than picked off a list
05 · Enquire

Enquire

price on enquiry · multi-season

Above Platinum we have no list left to read from, so this tier starts as a conversation rather than a number. Tell us what you want the partnership to do and we will scope it.

Start the conversation

Everything in Platinum, plus what it can't cover

  • A commitment across several seasons at once
  • Sole sponsor in your industry, held exclusively
  • Presenting partner on the team identity itself
  • Co-branded builds and commissioned projects
  • Early input on season planning and outreach
  • Anything else we can agree to and deliver
02 · in-kind / ~/other-ways

Equipment and hours help as much as money

Equipment, materials and expertise are worth as much to us as cash. We place in-kind sponsors at the tier matching what they have given.

Equipment & materials

Tools, fabrication help, prototyping supplies, or printing resources.

  • 3D printer filament
  • Electronics components
  • Workshop supplies
Professional support

Volunteer mentorship in engineering, software, comms, or planning.

  • Robotics engineering
  • Soft robotics research
  • CAD & manufacturing
  • Presentation coaching
03 · impact / where it goes

Where your support goes

Registration

Competition registration fees

Parts

Prototype parts, sensors, replacement hardware

Outreach

Presentation materials and community outreach demos

Resources

Team learning resources and practice field upgrades

04 · the details / ~/fine-print

The details

  • You are contracting with Lebob Robotics, an unincorporated not-for-profit association the students started and run themselves. Most of our members attend Perth Modern School, though no school or host organisation sits between you and us.
  • All amounts are in AUD and exclude GST. All sponsorships are invoiced under our ABN, 41 535 175 584.
  • Shirt logos have to be locked in before the season print run. Ask us for this season's cutoff date before you commit to a Silver tier or above.
  • Logo placement on the robot is subject to the competition's branding rules, which set limits on size and position.
  • The Enquire tier is scoped case by case and normally runs across multiple seasons. Industry exclusivity is first come, first served, and we confirm the scope in writing before anything is invoiced.
  • Tiers apply from the 2026–27 season onward. The organisations who backed us before we had a tier structure are listed permanently as Founding Supporters.
