About

Who we are

LeBob is a FIRST LEGO League team from Perth Modern School, Western Australia. We compete internationally in FLL, engineering autonomous robots and researching real-world problems. For the 2025/26 Unearthed season, we're building mission robots on LEGO Spike Prime and developing SoftSense — a soft-robotic manipulator arm designed for underwater ROV operations.


We design in Onshape, print on Bambu Lab, code in Python, and document everything. The workshop is home.
