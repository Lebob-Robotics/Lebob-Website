The grid

The cockatoo sits on 88 by 90 squares. Call one square u. The wordmark sits on the same squares.

The bird and ROBOTICS both run one u per pixel. LEBOB runs three.
Grey squares mark the bird's 1u lattice over one patch of the crest. Red squares behind LEBOB mark 3u.

Work in u and every number stays whole. Clear space runs 8u. The gap between bird and wordmark runs 16u across and 12u stacked, and ROBOTICS carries 7u between letters. Scale the logo from a 25 mm sticker to a 3 m banner and those ratios hold.

One number refuses to land: LEBOB measures 114u across and ROBOTICS 112u. Flush edges need LEBOB's pixel to be a multiple of 7u, which would push the word to 266u beside an 88u bird. The two lines sit centred with 1u to spare on each side.

The pixel font

Press Start 2P belongs in the logo and the team number. Keep it out of everything else.

CodeMan38 drew Press Start 2P for an arcade screen at 8 pixels, in one weight. Set a sponsor proposal in it and your reader gives up on the second paragraph. Set the logo in it and you get the face the team already used, on the same squares as the bird.

IBM Plex Sans carries the reading. IBM commissioned it for engineering documentation, which is close enough to a parts list and a grant application to cover what this team writes.

This page never loads Press Start 2P as a web font, so you cannot set live text in it here even by accident. Every pixel letter on the page came out of an SVG.

Beside sponsors

Hold the 8u clear space, then size each sponsor logo to the cap height of LEBOB. Matching bounding boxes instead lets a wide sponsor logo shout over the team.

Clear space and size

Keep 8u clear on all four sides. One character cell of ROBOTICS measures the same 8u, so you can eyeball it off the wordmark.

Nothing enters that margin. No type, no sponsor logo, no photo edge.

Briefing a printer

Send the SVG and this page. Tell them two spot colours, no anti-aliasing on the bird, and no scaling below the minimum in the table above.

The flat right cheek

The bird's right side ends flat. Andre drew it that way. Earlier versions of this page called it a canvas clip and costed out a dozen red squares to round the cheek off, which would redraw the mark the team has used since the start. Leave it flat. Every file here does.
