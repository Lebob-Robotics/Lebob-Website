Who we are

Lebob is an international FIRST LEGO League team based in Perth, Western Australia. We engineer robots, research soft-robotic mechanisms, and ship our work as documentation others can learn from.


Eight members, two mentors, one shared workshop. Our goal is turning ideas into real, working prototypes through teamwork, and then sharing them with the rest of the engineering world.


"the workshop is home."


We compete in the Unearthed season and run the SoftSense innovation project · a soft gripper for ROV manipulation. The goal: software, mechanisms, and a team that work well and feel right.
