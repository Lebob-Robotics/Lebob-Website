The result

We took this portfolio and our robot, Shelob, to the FIRST Tech Challenge Western Australia Qualifier at Peter Carnley Anglican Community School in Perth on 15 November 2026. It was our first FTC event of the BIOBUZZ season.

  • Inspire Award winner, the top judged award in FIRST Tech Challenge.
  • Winning Alliance, as first pick for team 37615 Hive Tide. We won all three playoff matches, 159 to 30, 169 to 68 and 132 to 86 in the final.
  • Ranked 5th of 15 after qualification, with a 3–2 record. Overall record 6–2–0.
  • First on advancement points, with 132: 60 for judging, 40 for playoffs, 20 for alliance selection and 12 for qualification. That made us the first team to advance from the event to the FIRST Tech Challenge Australia Championship at Macquarie University on 5 and 6 December 2026.

Official results are on FIRST Tech Challenge Events, and the advancement points are in the event advancement report.
